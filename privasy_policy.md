## 1. Overview
This Privacy Policy explains how **Electric Flow** ("the Game", "we", "our") handles user data and information when you install and play our puzzle game on Android devices.

---

## 2. Information Collected and Stored Locally
Electric Flow operates primarily offline. All core game progress and user settings are stored locally on your device:
- **Game Progress:** Highest level unlocked, level completion states, star ratings, and win streaks.
- **Player Economy:** Earned in-game coins, daily rewards, and mission completions.
- **Game Preferences:** Music and sound effect volume, visual themes, symbol preferences, and daily challenge notification settings.
- **Purchase Status:** Local entitlement flag indicating whether the optional "Remove Ads" purchase is active.
- **Privacy Consent:** Record of your acknowledgment of this Privacy Policy.

---

## 3. Daily Challenge Notifications
If enabled, Electric Flow may schedule local reminders via Android's notification system to notify you when a new Daily Challenge is available.
- On Android 13 (API 33) and above, the application requests standard `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` runtime permission.
- The `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` permission is utilized solely to ensure that scheduled daily challenge alarms are restored after your device restarts.
- **Control:** You can turn notifications on or off at any time directly within the in-game Settings menu or via Android System Settings.

---

## 4. Advertising
The free version of Electric Flow uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements:
- **Advertising Partners:** Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, approximate location, ad performance metrics, and diagnostic logs under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Ad Removal:** Players can permanently remove all advertisements through the optional in-game "Remove Ads" purchase.

---

## 5. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)
Electric Flow offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to remove all advertisements:
- All financial transactions are securely processed directly by **Google Play Billing**.
- We never collect, transmit, or store your credit card or payment credentials.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy
Electric Flow is designed for general and family audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
