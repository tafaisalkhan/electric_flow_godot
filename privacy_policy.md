# Electric Flow — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 30, 2026

## 1. Overview
This Privacy Policy explains how **Electric Flow** ("the Game", "we", "our") handles user data and information when you install and play our puzzle game on Android devices.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent, secure gaming experience.

---

## 2. Information Collected and Stored Locally
Electric Flow operates primarily offline. All core game progress and user settings are stored locally on your device:
- **Game Progress:** Highest level unlocked, level completion states, star ratings, and win streaks.
- **Player Economy:** Earned in-game coins, daily rewards, and mission completions.
- **Game Preferences:** Music and sound effect volume, visual themes, symbol preferences, and daily challenge notification settings.
- **Purchase Status:** Local entitlement flag indicating whether the optional "Remove Ads" purchase is active.
- **Privacy Consent:** Record of your acknowledgment of this Privacy Policy.

This local data remains on your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing the application's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the application.

---

## 3. Daily Challenge Notifications
If enabled, Electric Flow may schedule local reminders via Android's notification system to notify you when a new Daily Challenge is available.
- On Android 13 (API 33) and above, the application requests the standard `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` runtime permission.
- The `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` permission is utilized solely to ensure that scheduled daily challenge alarms are restored after your device restarts.
- **Control:** You can turn notifications on or off at any time directly within the in-game Settings menu or via Android System Settings.

---

## 4. Advertising
The free version of Electric Flow uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements:
- **Advertising Partners:** Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), approximate location inferred from IP address, ad performance metrics, and diagnostic logs in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Ad Removal:** Players can permanently remove all banner and interstitial advertisements through the optional in-game "Remove Ads" purchase.

---

## 5. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)
Electric Flow offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to remove all advertisements:
- All financial transactions are securely processed directly by **Google Play Billing**.
- We never collect, transmit, or store your credit card, bank account, or payment credentials.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy
Electric Flow is designed for general and family audiences.
- We do not collect personal information (such as real names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses) from any user, including children under 13.
- Parents and guardians are encouraged to monitor their children's device usage and configure Google Play parental controls for in-app purchases.

---

## 7. Cloud Save & Google Play Games (Optional)
If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games Services:
- Your game progress and achievements may be synchronized with your Google Play Games account via Google's official APIs.
- This data is managed directly by Google under their privacy policies.

## 8. Data Security and Retention
- We do not operate external web servers that store personal user profiles.
- Any network requests made by third-party SDKs (such as Google Play or Google AdMob) utilize encrypted HTTPS connections.
- Uninstalling the game completely removes all local save files from your device.
